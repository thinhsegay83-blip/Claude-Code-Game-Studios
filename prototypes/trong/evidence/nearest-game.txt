Nearest game: Undertale; this one differs in: no fixed FIGHT/ACT/ITEM/MERCY menu (each creature has its own verbs: Nói khẽ, Sưởi cạnh, Gỡ mảnh sắt, Gỡ một con), the soul is two orbiting heartbeats not a heart, grazing bullets fills Cộng Hưởng that doubles the next action, a companion telegraphs each wave (Linh nghe nhịp, Kaelen hét), the arena widens when you have companions, the world stays visible behind the fight, and every choice writes into shared story variables (Lumen/Memoria economy, sinh thái, sổ nợ of Linh) that reprice the market and rewrite item text.
